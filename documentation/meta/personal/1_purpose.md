# purpose

**in short.** why this repository exists, what it's trying to do, and what it
isn't. like every personal chapter, it's written in the first person, because
it's about who's writing, and from where, so that a reader can weigh the rest.

**builds on:** nothing.

it defines no concepts of its own; the index of those is
[concepts.md](../../concepts.md).

## what this is

a place where i model ideas i care about or find interesting, and write down
the perspective behind them: how they're framed, what they mean, and how they
might be adapted and put to use. it documents perspective as much as ideas.

its first use is mine: writing a thought down well enough that it can be
checked. once it's written, others, people or AI, can help debug it, by
finding the flaws, the fallacies and the places it doesn't hold, or by
grounding it in what's already known.

## what it's trying to do

- **pull things apart**: tell what something is from what it isn't, especially
  from its nearest neighbours
- **reframe**: little here, if anything, is new. the hope is to describe
  familiar ideas in a light that reaches someone they haven't reached before,
  and to jar loose a perspective that's stuck
- **make meaning usable**: communicate semantics clearly enough to be put to
  concrete use, with economic or cultural value
- **show the reasoning**: so the models can be tested, not just believed

## potential value

- for a reader: a clearer or fresher view of ideas they half know
- for someone building things: models with their semantics made explicit,
  ready for tests and implementations
- for someone learning: ideas in dependency order, in plain words
- for me: a record of my thinking that can be debugged

## what it isn't

- **novel**: most of these ideas exist somewhere, under other names, and the
  "related" notes and the crosswalk point to them
- **expert work**: i'm not an expert in the fields it touches (mathematics,
  linguistics, ontology, computer science, teaching). where it uses their
  terms, it tries to use them as they're meant, and says when something is
  recalled rather than checked
- **finished, or a doctrine**: the perspective is still forming, and some of
  it is still blurry

## who's writing

i'm trying to be a documentarian of perspective (not of video), and an
effective communicator, especially of meaning that can be put to use. what
documenting perspective means, apart from me, is in
[documentation](../documentation/1_foundations.md).

## reading it, given all that

- trust the structure where it's been checked, and the perspective only as far
  as it persuades you
- disagreement is the point: the "case against" sections, and "open to
  challenge", in the approach, are where to start
- if something here jars a stuck view loose, it's done its job, even if the
  details turn out wrong
